# 🌱 Reading #2: Curry et al. (2025) Alignment Problem
Curry, N., McEnery, T., & Brookes, G. (2025) A question of alignment – AI, GenAI and applied linguistics, _Annual Review of Applied Linguistics_ 45, 315-336. 

+ Focus: Examining the "Alignment Gap" between AI/GenAI and the foundational values of Applied Linguistics (AL)

## 1. Context: The AI "Flexion Point"
+ Flexion Point (2022): The mainstream rise of GenAI (e.g., ChatGPT) represents a shift where technology's utility significantly improved, causing both excitement and fear.
+ Shift in Approach: Move from Symbolic AI (rule-based, e.g., ELIZA) to Statistical AI (probabilistic modeling based on large-scale corpora), which now drives Large Language Models (LLMs).
## 2. The Core Problem: The Alignment Gap
The authors argue that there is a fundamental misalignment between how machines process data and how Applied Linguistics understands language and knowledge.
### Epistemological Gap (Ways of Knowing):

+ AL: Knowledge is reflexive, socialized, and context-dependent.
+ AI: Knowledge is a product of probabilistic pattern recognition.
+ Risks: Leads to "hallucinations" (false data) and "epistemicide" (standardization that erases non-dominant or indigenous knowledge systems).

### Ontological Gap (Nature of Language):
+ AL: Language is an actively negotiated social practice.
+ AI: Language is often reduced to fixed ontologies (structured, machine-readable formats) to simplify complexity.
+ Result: "Descriptive Poverty"—AI fails to capture cultural, historical, and situational nuances.

## 3. Ethical Challenges & The "Black Box"
+ Transparency: Commercial AI is often a "Black Box"; it provides answers without showing the reasoning process, lacking the explanatory adequacy required for academic research.
+ Embedded Bias: AI models can amplify societal prejudices (race, gender, culture) found in their training data.
+ Global Justice: High environmental costs (energy/water usage for server farms) raise ethical concerns regarding sustainability.

## 4. Impact on Language Education
+ Affordances: Offers personalized learning, instant feedback, and administrative efficiency (lesson/quiz generation).
+ Threats:
  + De-skilling: Over-reliance on AI may cause students to bypass the essential process of learning.
  + Purity Myth: GenAI often reinforces "Standard" language varieties, potentially marginalizing Global Englishes and linguistic diversity.

## 5. Conclusion: Augmented Intelligence over Automation

The authors conclude that the goal should not be the "automation" of expertise but the augmentation of human ability.

+ Augmented Intelligence: Technology should extend and amplify human cognition, not replace it.
+ Human as Final Arbiter: The researcher must remain the final arbiter of value, maintaining control over data interpretation through a reflexive lens.
+ Critical AI Literacy: Disciplinary guidelines must prioritize nuanced human knowledge over machine efficiency to bridge the alignment gap.

## Pedagogical Implications & Discussion

Drawing on the core arguments regarding the "Alignment Gap" presented in the sources, here are three critical questions that this paper poses to those majoring in English Education:

1. Protecting the Learning Process: How can we redesign English language tasks to ensure that students do not bypass the essential "process of learning"—such as searching, analyzing, and negotiating meaning—in favor of the instant, probabilistic answers provided by Generative AI?

2. Addressing Linguistic Diversity: Since GenAI often reinforces "standard" language varieties and fixed ontologies, what pedagogical strategies can educators use to prevent machine-driven "epistemicide" and continue promoting the field's commitment to Global Englishes and linguistic diversity?

3. The Role of the Human Arbiter: In an era of opaque "Black Box" algorithms, how can English teachers move away from the "quest for automation" and instead develop the critical AI literacy necessary to function as the "final arbiter" who uses technology to amplify and extend human cognition rather than replace it?
