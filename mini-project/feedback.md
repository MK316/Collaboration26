## Project #1: Simple and learner-friendly vocabulary support

### Strength Keywords
vocabulary reinforcement · repetition · pronunciation practice · sound-picture-meaning connection · visual support · classroom applicability · retry design · level differentiation

### Feedback Summary
Project #1 was consistently recognized for its strong practical value in vocabulary learning, particularly in supporting repeated exposure, individualized review, and multimodal engagement through sound, meaning, and visual cues. Peers appreciated how naturally the app addresses a real classroom challenge, namely providing meaningful vocabulary reinforcement beyond limited class time. Its intuitive design and immediate classroom applicability were viewed as major strengths, while suggestions such as adaptive feedback, selective visual support, and richer retry cycles point to promising directions for further refinement into an even more interactive learner-centered tool.

### Peer feedback radar profile
Project #1 demonstrates balanced strength across all evaluation areas, particularly in educational usefulness, pedagogical thinking, and clear app design. Its strongest quality lies in translating a simple learning idea into an accessible and instructionally meaningful tool that could be readily adopted in real classroom settings. For future improvement, adding more adaptive feedback features and richer learner interaction could further deepen engagement while preserving its simplicity and usability.

## Project #2: Practical grammar support for real classrooms

### Strength Keywords
sentence structure · grammar awareness · verb patterns · self-directed learning · feedback · review · classroom practicality · personalized practice


### Feedback Summary
Project #2 was especially valued for its clear pedagogical focus on sentence structure and grammar awareness, transforming what is often treated as abstract grammatical knowledge into a more accessible and self-directed learning experience. Peer feedback highlighted the app’s broad classroom applicability across different school levels, its usefulness for both guided instruction and independent review, and its potential to strengthen learners’ understanding of sentence patterns through repeated practice and structured feedback. Particularly noteworthy was its ability to connect explicit grammar instruction with meaningful language use, suggesting strong foundations for further development through streamlined design, gamified interaction, and increased emphasis on productive application.

### Peer feedback radar profile
Project #2 shows particularly strong performance in app design and pedagogical grounding, suggesting a well-structured tool built on clear educational reasoning. Its major strength is its practical classroom orientation, connecting grammatical knowledge with learner practice in ways that are both usable and instructionally relevant across contexts. To strengthen the project further, simplifying information flow and incorporating more interactive or game-like learning elements could increase learner motivation and make the app even more dynamic.

## Project #3: Multi-layered reading support needing clearer learning boundaries

### Strength Keywords
scaffolded learning across levels · adaptability · API use · reading support · vocabulary information · self-study · learner autonomy · differentiated learning


### Feedback Summary
Project #3 stood out for its ambitious scope and sophisticated design, particularly in its adaptive level structure, rich informational support, and orientation toward learner autonomy. Peers saw strong potential in its capacity to promote differentiated learning, self-study, and personalized engagement with reading materials, making it especially valuable as a supplementary learning resource. At the same time, constructive feedback suggested that simplifying information density and clarifying learning boundaries or unit goals could strengthen classroom usability. Overall, the project was viewed as intellectually rich and forward-looking, with considerable potential as a flexible platform that bridges guided instruction and independent language development.

### Peer feedback radar profile
Project #3 received the strongest overall evaluation, especially in design, educational usefulness, completion, and pedagogical depth, reflecting an ambitious and thoughtfully developed learning platform. Its greatest strength is its rich, multi-layered support for differentiated learning, learner autonomy, and adaptable instructional use. Moving forward, clarifying learning boundaries, simplifying content density, and organizing activities into more focused learning units would make its strong educational potential even more accessible and classroom-ready.
