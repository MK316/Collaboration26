
# Sentence-by-Sentence TTS Reader

## 1. App Purpose
This app is designed to support reading and listening practice for language learners.  
It helps learners focus on one sentence at a time while still seeing the whole passage.

## 2. Target Learners
This app is intended for English language learners who want to practice reading, listening, and pronunciation by themselves.  
It can be useful for middle school, high school, or university learners depending on the text they use.

## 3. Data or Content Used
The app uses learner-provided text.  
Users can paste any short passage, such as a reading text, dialogue, short story, or study material.

## 4. What Users Can Learn or Do
Users can:
- split a passage into sentence units
- choose one sentence at a time
- listen to the selected sentence through text-to-speech
- locate the sentence in the full passage
- practice sentence-by-sentence reading and listening

## 5. Classroom or Self-Study Use
This app can be used for:
- reading aloud practice
- shadowing practice
- sentence-by-sentence listening
- self-paced reading support
- checking sentence location in context

It is especially useful for learners who study independently because they can use their own passage and repeat the activity as many times as they want.

## 6. App Design Description
The app consists of the following parts:
- text input area
- sentence segmentation function
- sentence selection dropdown
- text-to-speech audio output
- full passage display
- sentence highlighting in context

This design helps learners move between sentence-level practice and passage-level understanding.

## 7. Difficulties or Limitations
One limitation is that sentence splitting is based mainly on punctuation, so it may not always work perfectly with abbreviations or unusual text.  
Another limitation is that the app currently uses only a simple text-to-speech voice and does not record learner speech.

## 8. Further Improvement
This app could be improved by adding:
- multiple language options
- adjustable speech speed
- learner voice recording
- repeat and auto-play functions
- vocabulary support for difficult words
